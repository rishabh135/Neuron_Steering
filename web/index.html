The exported vectors come from BF16 columns and BF16 calibration. On a quantized GGUF they are a port, not a measurement of that model's neurons. See README.md for the full sequence.

What counts as evidence

  1. Zero-dose sham: all three branches must match token for token.
  2. Each neuron alone at 0.25, 0.50 and 1.00 before any mixture.
  3. Blinded scoring of tone, false-premise agreement, correctness, format and coherence.
  4. Repeat on Q8_0 and Q4_K_M; report differences rather than assuming equivalence.
